Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Is JS Mens PG a men's-only PG?

Yes. JS Mens PG Hostel is a men's-only paying guest (PG) accommodation in Ramapuram, Chennai.

Where is JS Mens PG located?

Plot No. 78, EVP Santhosh Nagar, Ramapuram, Chennai 600089, near Mount-Poonamallee Road, with convenient access to DLF offices, L&T, LTIMindtree, MIOT Hospital and SRM University.

Is it near SRM University?

Yes. SRM University in Ramapuram is close by, along with MIOT and SRM hospitals.

What room-sharing options are available?

2-sharing, 2-sharing Plus, 3-sharing, 3-sharing Plus, 4-sharing and executive 1BHK units, each furnished with beds and wardrobes. Current availability, rent and deposit are shared on enquiry.

What security facilities are available?

Face-scan entry to the PG, CCTV coverage and controlled access.

Is food available?

Food on order service is available. Meals are not a fixed daily included service; food can be ordered when needed.

Is Wi-Fi and laundry available?

Yes. Wi-Fi is available in rooms and common areas, and washing machines are available for everyday laundry.

How can someone enquire about a room?

Call or WhatsApp +91 7305169396, or use any Enquire on WhatsApp button on this website.
